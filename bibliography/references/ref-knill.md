# [Knill23] Knill — Some Fundamental Theorems in Mathematics

**Oliver Knill**, *Some Fundamental Theorems in Mathematics*, revision of
25 June 2023.
<https://people.math.harvard.edu/~knill/graphgeometry/papers/fundamental.pdf>

An expository survey of 272 theorems, chosen for being elegant, beautiful or
useful rather than deep, and ordered by when they were written down. The
Jordan curve theorem is its §48, and that entry is where the benchmark
statement came from.
