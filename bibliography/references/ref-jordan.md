# [Jor87] Jordan — Cours d'analyse

**Camille Jordan**, *Cours d'analyse de l'École Polytechnique*, second
edition, 1887.

Where the theorem is first stated. The proof given there is famously
incomplete, and the gap is not a slip — it is the point at which intuition
about drawn curves stops being a guide to arbitrary continuous ones. That the
statement needed eighteen more years to be proved is the best evidence that
it is a theorem rather than an observation.
