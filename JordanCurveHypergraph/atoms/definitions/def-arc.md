# Definition — arc

The other half of the vocabulary: a curve with two ends rather than none. The
distinction carries the whole separation argument — an arc does not separate
the plane, a [[def-jordan-curve|Jordan curve]] does, and the proof works by
cutting the curve into two arcs and playing them against each other.

## Statement

> [!definition] Arc
> An *arc* is a subset of the plane homeomorphic to the unit interval $[0,1]$.
