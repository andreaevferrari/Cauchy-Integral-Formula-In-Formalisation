# Definition — region

The standing hypothesis of the subject. Openness lets a function be
differentiated at every point of the set; connectedness is what makes a
local conclusion propagate to the whole of it.

## Statement

> [!definition] Region
> A *region* is a non-empty [[def-open-set|open]] [[def-connected|connected]] subset $G \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ ([[def-complex-numbers|complex numbers]]).
