# Definition — circle, open disk, closed disk

Three sets that recur in every statement below, fixed here once so that the
statements can name them without repeating the inequality.

## Statement

> [!definition] Circle and disks
> For $a \in \mathbb{C}$ and $r > 0$, with $|\cdot|$ the [[def-modulus-and-parts|modulus]],
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> C[a,r] &= \{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - a| = r \}, \\
> D[a,r] &= \{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - a| < r \}, \\
> \overline{D}[a,r] &= \{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - a| \le r \}
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> are the *circle*, the *open disk* and the *closed disk* of centre $a$ and radius $r$. Unless said otherwise, $C[a,r]$ carries the counter-clockwise orientation.
