# Definition — the length of a path

## Statement

> [!definition] Length
> The *length* of a [[def-piecewise-smooth-path|piecewise smooth path]] $\gamma : [a,b] \to \mathbb{C}$ is
> $$
> \operatorname{length}(\gamma) \; = \; \int_a^b |\gamma'(t)| \, dt .
> $$
