# Definition — the inside of a simple closed path

What "$w$ is enclosed by $\gamma$" means. That the complement splits into
exactly two pieces, one of them bounded, is not obvious and is not proved here — it is [[thm-jordan-curve|assumed]].

## Statement

> [!definition] Inside of a simple closed path
> Let $\gamma$ be a simple closed path in $\mathbb{C}$. By [[thm-jordan-curve]] its complement $\mathbb{C} \setminus \gamma$ has exactly two [[def-connected|connected components]], exactly one of which is bounded. That bounded component is the *inside* (or *interior*) of $\gamma$; a point of it is said to be *inside* $\gamma$.
